# Templates

## Weekly management self-review

```markdown
## Week of [Date]: Management review

### Team output
- Shipped / delivered: [list]
- Indicators off-trend: [indicator → action taken]

### Leverage
- High-leverage activities this week: [decisions unblocked, training, planning, feedback]
- Negative leverage caught: [meddling, delayed decisions, unprepared meetings]
- Decisions I'm holding that block others: [list → date I'll decide]

### People
- 1:1s held: [X/Y] (cadence still matched to TRM? [y/n, who to adjust])
- Delegated work monitored: [item → owner → last sample → status]
- Recognition given: [who, for what]
- Performance or motivation concerns: [who, can't vs. won't, next step]

### Next week
- What only I can do: [1–3 items]
- What I'll hand off: [items → owner]
```
